### Changed

- Map view: a sprite pixel drawn through a tile in the screen door, and a hover-lifted sprite pixel, keep their own color where the covering layer is in the map's color-math table: only the sprite part skips the blend, so a dim door pixel is the tile through color math at three quarters plus the sprite at one quarter, and a bright or lifted pixel is the pure sprite color (#936, owner rulings D60 B and D62 call 2 B; changes #866 and #891). Pixels outside the door blend as before. Evidence: synthetic tests over layers 1 to 3 at both priorities, both door parities, lifted pixels, the add, half-add, subtract and half-subtract modes, and a sub screen that is opaque or empty; on the vanilla ROM, one machine, in the 488 maps that build (1315 screens with sprites and a color-math table), with the layers 1 to 3 shown or hidden in each of the 8 combinations, no door pixel (up to 73388 with all three shown) lies under a layer in its map's color-math table, so vanilla output in those states is unchanged; the other 24 map slots do not build in the map view.
