### Changed

- Maps view: a sprite pixel drawn through a tile in the screen door, and a hover-lifted sprite pixel, no longer take part in colour blending, so the sprite keeps its own colour there even when the covering layer is in the map's colour-math table (#936, owner rulings D60 B and D62 call 2 B; changes #866 and #891). Pixels outside the door blend as before. Evidence: synthetic tests over both door parities and the add, half-add, subtract and half-subtract modes; on the vanilla ROM, one machine, none of the 73388 door pixels in the 488 maps that build (1315 screens with sprites) lies under a layer in its map's colour-math table, so vanilla output is unchanged; the other 24 map slots do not build in the map view.
