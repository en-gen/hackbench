### Changed

- Standard objects $34 (CODE_0DBA4C) and the variant B staircase (CODE_0DC3D8): CODE_0DBA4C reads its CPX threshold and X mask from the handler, CODE_0DC3D8 reads its X mask, and both check every opcode they read through and that its operand is inside the ROM, refusing the object with a recorded reason otherwise (#519). CODE_0DC3D8 also refuses when one of these hard-coded bytes differs from SMWDisX (bank_0D.asm:4933-4975): INC _0 at +22, CMP #$01 at +39, BEQ and its displacement at +55, or the target of any of its nine JSRs, compared in the handler's bank (#762). Still unpinned, left for a follow-up: in CODE_0DC3D8 the JMP at +24, BNE at +41, BPL at +81 and the LSR and loop bookkeeping bytes; in CODE_0DBA4C its JSR targets and loop bytes. A test on the vanilla and magic ROMs checks neither port refuses there. Every refusal is tested on synthetic ROMs, no corpus needed; the 6 corpus ROMs and 101 patched hacks (one machine, 2026-10-10) hold these bytes as SMWDisX on 105 of 107, and the other 2 were already refused at the AND at +8, so rendering is unchanged.
