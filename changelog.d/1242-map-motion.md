### Added

- Map view: a play button on maps with a scroll routine. It runs the map's own scroll code from the ROM (the level loader, then the game's per-frame screen and scroll routines on the 65816 core) and shows what it moves: Layer 2 shifts against the map, and a screen-sized frame follows the camera when the routine moves it. Maps that move by themselves ($009) need no input; maps that wait for Mario standing on Layer 2 ($1EF) are shown as if he is, and say so; a routine that moves nothing, that moves sideways, that depends on where Mario is, or that the interpreter refuses, says why and moves nothing. Stop restores the at-rest picture. Evidence: synthetic carts in CI (a changed table step changes the range; no command reports no routine; refused code moves nothing); vanilla ROM, one machine, 2026-11-07: $009 matches a Mesen capture on 7,999 of 7,999 frames, and all 512 slots run with no loader or interpreter refusal (#1242).
