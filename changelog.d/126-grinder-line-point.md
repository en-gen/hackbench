### Changed

- Maps view: line-guided grinders ($67) are now drawn as the game shows them on its third frame, where the line walk has put the grinder on its line, not at their spawn spot or the probe-corner spot between (#126). Why: the $67 arm of the line-guide dispatch draws the grinder (JSR CODE_01DC0B) before it jumps to the line step (JMP CODE_01D74D) (SMWDisX bank_01.asm:12194-12204), and the line step stores the on-track position into the sprite's X and Y (bank_01.asm:12054-12061), so the first draw is at the off-track spawn spot, the second at the probe-corner spot the first line step snapped to (3 to 12 px off the line), and the third on the line; evidence: a static trace of the vanilla ROM, plus one Mesen capture (slot $1F, first grinder, sprite slot 7), which holds two MAINs: the position in WRAM after the second MAIN, (431,336), is the interpreter's third-pass draw (the earlier snapshots read (415,336) after INIT and (419,340) after the first MAIN); not a capture of every grinder. Effect: the position moves up to 12 px for 17 of the 20 vanilla grinders (maps $01A and $01F), every one landing on the edge of its line tile, and the pose is the third frame's; evidence: the interpreter run over all 20 vanilla placements, one machine, checked against the capture for that one grinder only; 3 of the 20 are not drawn at all yet (#793). Other line-guided sprites are unchanged by construction: the rule applies to sprite id $67 only (tested on synthetic carts for another id, including one whose main is the grinder handler); no vanilla or hack ROM was compared before and after for them (#792). Grinders in a ROM where LineGrinder's pinned entry bytes (bank_01.asm:11837-11846) or the $67 dispatch (bank_01.asm:12182-12204) differ from vanilla's are unchanged too: the dispatch's opcodes, immediates and branch displacements are pinned by digest, and each JSR/JSL/JMP target is pinned to vanilla's routine (CODE_01DBD4, CODE_01DC0B, MarioSprInteractRt twice, CODE_03C263, CODE_01D74D), read through the FastROM mirror; evidence: the check matched on 25 of 152 ROMs probed (3 vanilla-code ROMs and 22 hacks; the runner refuses the other 127 before this check), one machine, 2026-10-10, and refused on synthetic fixtures with any pinned byte or any target operand byte changed. A Maps-view Playwright case pins map $01A's first grinder at its third-pass position and frame (vanilla ROM).
