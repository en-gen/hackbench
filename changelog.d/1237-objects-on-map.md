### Added

- Map view: the saved Objects of a map are found and selectable, read-only (#1237). Hover shows a faint dashed outline and a light tint; a click selects the whole Object with one outline and a light tint, never a box per tile; on a selected Object the edge segments can be hovered and selected, Ctrl+click adding more, and a run of slope tiles at one angle, a single tile included, is one monotone segment. Objects are found from the stored pieces (a covered Object whole; touching Objects kept apart, except one platform above another when their bodies are 2 rows tall or less) and from visible tiles, and a visible edge tile dropped into an Object's body by a later piece stops that Object's match, from the definitions in `meta/objects/`; a map with per-map tiles says so in plain words. Evidence: synthetic cases for the matching rule, a swept hill, a four-platform stack in 60 piece orders (a platform a later piece wholly hides is not offered) and the planted drop of the piece split; vanilla `$103` screen 0 finds its four platforms; recognition on the largest vanilla map `$10A` (32 screens, 272 stored pieces) takes 5.9 ms and the whole read 8.3 ms (perf bench medians at 7e0f7b2e, 20 samples, one machine; `app.open-maps` shows no regression against develop).
