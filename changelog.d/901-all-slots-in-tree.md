### Changed

- Maps (#901): the Maps tree now lists every pointer-table slot, 512 on vanilla, and the Unassigned folder includes the placeholder slots. The slots that only hold the shared placeholder room (277 on vanilla, the count read from the ROM) show as dimmed orphans, and their tooltip says the slot uses the shared placeholder room and how many slots share it. The label counts the top-level rows and the placeholders apart, for example `Unassigned (101, 277 placeholder)` on vanilla with no groups. The map count, entry maps, sub areas and bonus rooms are unchanged. Evidence: synthetic tests for the 12-sharing and 9-sharing cases (`MapTree.placeholder.synthetic.test.ts`), and a corpus test over the six corpus ROMs (`MapTree.placeholder.corpus.test.ts`); Playwright spec `map-placeholder.spec.cjs` measured the Unassigned folder expanding with all 512 slots listed at 38-44 ms across 3 runs (headless Chromium, vanilla ROM, one machine). A drag highlight no longer sticks when the dragged row scrolls out of view. A slot with no level pointer at all (a hacked ROM can have them; none in vanilla or the corpus ROMs, synthetic tests only) is listed too, as a row with no data that opens nothing and no group can take, with its own count in the folder label (`with no data`).
