### Changed

- Maps (#901): the Unassigned folder now lists every pointer-table slot, 512 on vanilla. The slots that only hold the shared placeholder room (277 on vanilla, the count read from the ROM) show as dimmed orphans, and their tooltip says the slot uses the shared placeholder room and how many slots share it. The label counts the top-level rows and the placeholders apart, for example `Unassigned (101, 277 placeholder)` on vanilla with no groups. The map count, entry maps, sub areas and bonus rooms are unchanged. Evidence: synthetic tests for the 12-sharing and 9-sharing cases (`MapTree.placeholder.synthetic.test.ts`), and a corpus test over the six corpus ROMs (`MapTree.placeholder.corpus.test.ts`); Playwright spec `map-placeholder.spec.cjs` measured the Unassigned folder expanding with all 512 slots listed at 33-43 ms across 3 runs (headless Chromium, vanilla ROM, one machine).
