### Changed

- Maps (#901): the Maps tree now lists every pointer-table slot, 512 on vanilla, so none is hidden. The Unassigned folder holds the maps no overworld entry reaches, including the slots that share the ROM's most common level data (277 on vanilla, the count read from the ROM). The map count, entry maps, sub areas and bonus rooms are unchanged. Evidence: synthetic tests for the 12-sharing and 9-sharing cases (`MapTree.slots.synthetic.test.ts`) and a corpus test over the six corpus ROMs (`MapTree.slots.corpus.test.ts`). A drag highlight no longer sticks when the dragged row scrolls out of view. How a slot with no usable level data is shown ("New") is in the #949 entry.
