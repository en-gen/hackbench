### Fixed

- Maps (#860): the initial Layer 1 (foreground) camera height and Layer 2 (background) height of a map are now read from the entrance the game uses. The overworld or a screen exit without the secondary flag enters a map by its primary entrance (the `DATA_05F000`.. tables); only a map entered solely by secondary-entrance screen exits uses a secondary entrance, the lowest-numbered one (bank_05.asm:7111-7162, 7300-7337), and a vertical map's high byte then comes from that entrance's `DATA_05FC00` (:7147-7148). Before, a secondary entrance's row was read for any `$1xx` map that one aims at, and the entrance was found by bit 0 of `DATA_05FC00`; the destination is bit 8 of the entrance index, then `DATA_05F800` (:7103-7118). Where the exit graph is unavailable (a hack that replaces the submap-flag code) the primary entrance is read, never a secondary one. Evidence: synthetic ROMs, all 512 entrance indexes and every `DATA_05FC00` value; on the vanilla ROM, one machine, 11 maps change their chosen entrance and only `$110` changes its camera Y (0 to 192, Layer 1 and Layer 2); the reference-path camera changes on 6 maps of the four corpus hacks, which the shipped view does not show.
