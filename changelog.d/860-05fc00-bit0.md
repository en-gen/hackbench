### Fixed

- Maps (#860): the initial Layer 1 (foreground) camera height and Layer 2 (background) height of a map are now read from the entrance the game uses. The overworld or a main-entrance screen exit enters a map by its main entrance (DATA_05F000.. tables); only a map entered solely by secondary-entrance screen exits uses a secondary entrance, the lowest-numbered one (bank_05.asm:7111-7162, 7300-7337). Before, every `$1xx` map was read from a secondary entrance, and the entrance was found with bit 0 of DATA_05FC00 as the map's high byte; the destination is bit 8 of the entrance index, then DATA_05F800 (bank_05.asm:7103-7118). Where the exit graph is unavailable (a hack that replaces the submap-flag code) the main entrance is read, never a secondary one. Evidence: synthetic ROMs, all 512 entrance indexes and every DATA_05FC00 value; on the vanilla ROM, on one machine, the maps whose drawn entrance or camera heights change are listed in the PR (map `$110` among them).
