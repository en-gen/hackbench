### Changed

- Maps view: sprites $E3 and $E2 are now drawn as the ring of 10 pieces they spawn, not a numbered marker (#983; cluster OAM, related #839). Why: both ids are cluster spawns (SMWDisX bank_02.asm:5640-5677, 5669-5719) that make ten type-4 cluster sprites round the entry's position; the map view runs the ROM's own spawn and draw (CODE_02AB11, CODE_02FA98 at 16290-16429, per cluster slot from the loop at 15950-15958 and its type 4 dispatch entry, 15969) on the core, as it does for the castle flames (#894), and reads the OAM they write. The ring is one sprite at its real stream index, 80 px from the entry's position (radius $50 at bank_02.asm:16366, 16384; the sine table CircleCoords peaks at $0100, bank_07.asm:1012, 1045), 28.125 degrees between pieces (40 of 512 per piece, DATA_02FA84 bank_02.asm:16282-16288, 16333-16343; ten pieces, 5678-5679, 5688-5689, 5711, so an open arc of about 253 degrees), 16 x 16 (16665-16666), CGRAM row 9 and priority 3 (attribute 16545, 16653-16660), in OAM 76-85 (DATA_02FF50, 16907-16910; written by CODE_02FC8D and CODE_02FD48, 16531-16569, 16627-16666). Rendering choices: the map shows the spawn state, ring angle 0 (the ring turns in the game: $E2 counter-clockwise, $E3 clockwise, 512 frames a turn: step +1 or -1 a frame into a 9-bit angle, bank_02.asm:5673-5677, 16299-16310, and angle 0 is below the centre with 128 to the right, 16336-16362, 16364-16399); each ring is drawn alone; a vertical map keeps the marker with a stated reason; a changed routine or call path still draws the ring, flagged unverified, naming the part. The sprite names for $E2 and $E3 are now "Rotating ring of 10, counter-clockwise" and "Rotating ring of 10, clockwise" (they were "Enable Smoke Puffs" and "Disable Scroll (vertical)", uncited and not what the code does). Evidence: vanilla USA ROM, one machine, 2026-10-16; OAM 76-85 equal the Mesen frames of map $107 (layers_v5 windows screen_01 and screen_02) in position, tile, attribute and size, 10 of 10 each, at the angles those frames held; the other placements ($013, $0EC, $0EE for $E3; $0ED, $0F2, $0F8 for $E2; $1FB for both) have no hardware frame and are checked for geometry only (10 pieces, 80 px, 28.125 degrees).
