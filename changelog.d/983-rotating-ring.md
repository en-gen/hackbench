### Changed

- Maps view: sprites $E3 and $E2 are now drawn as the ring of 10 pieces they spawn, not a numbered marker (#983; cluster OAM, related #839). Why: both ids are cluster spawns (SMWDisX bank_02.asm:5640-5677, 5669-5719) that make ten type-4 cluster sprites round the entry's position; the map view runs the ROM's own spawn and draw (CODE_02AB11, CODE_02FA98) on the core, as it does for the castle flames (#894), and reads the OAM they write. The ring is one sprite at its real stream index, 80 px from the entry's position, 28.125 degrees between pieces (an open arc of about 253 degrees), 16 x 16, CGRAM row 9, priority 3. Rendering choices: the map shows the spawn state, ring angle 0 (the ring turns in the game: $E2 counter-clockwise, $E3 clockwise, 512 frames a turn); each ring is drawn alone; a vertical map keeps the marker with a stated reason; a changed routine or call path still draws the ring, flagged unverified, naming the part. The sprite names for $E2 and $E3 are now "Rotating ring of 10, counter-clockwise" and "Rotating ring of 10, clockwise" (they were "Enable Smoke Puffs" and "Disable Scroll (vertical)", uncited and not what the code does). Evidence: vanilla USA ROM, one machine, 2026-10-16; OAM 76-85 equal the Mesen frames of map $107 (layers_v5 windows screen_01 and screen_02) in position, tile, attribute and size, 10 of 10 each, at the angles those frames held; the other placements ($013, $0EC, $0EE for $E3; $0ED, $0F2, $0F8 for $E2; $1FB for both) have no hardware frame and are checked for geometry only (10 pieces, 80 px, 28.125 degrees). Details: docs/sprites/sprite-e2-e3-ring.md.
