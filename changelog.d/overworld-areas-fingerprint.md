### Changed

- Overworld areas: the area-list code recognizes the game's routines by SHA-256 fingerprints (operands masked, at least 24 unknown bytes each) instead of committed byte patterns, so no cart bytes sit in the source. The camera fingerprint now covers the whole read from its screen setup (41 bytes, 28 unknown). The warp, path and destination routines are found by their own fingerprint and must be reached by a JSL or JSR whose operand names them (read from the operand, no fingerprint over the short call sites), so a hack that moves one and retargets the call still resolves. Evidence: the derived area list and camera positions hash identically before and after on vanilla and its Lunar Magic resave, and the 4 hacks still refuse at the camera read (refusal text now cites the longer span), one machine; planted one-byte changes in each routine and call refuse on synthetic carts.
