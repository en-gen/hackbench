### Changed

- Overworld areas: the area-list code recognizes the game's routines by SHA-256 fingerprints (operands masked) instead of committed byte patterns, so no cart bytes sit in the source. The camera read is one fingerprint over 63 bytes (32 unknown after subtracting bytes the repo already states); the warp, path and destination routines keep their own (81, 40 and 41 unknown). Each routine must also be reached by a call of the right shape (read from the call's operands, no fingerprint over the short call sites): the destination JSL over a branch in the camera routine's bank, the path JSR after a branch and a push, and two warp-scan JSRs each after a compare and branch with different immediates. A stock copy left behind after a hack moves a routine is ignored when nothing calls it. Evidence, synthetic carts and one machine: every unmasked byte of each routine flipped refuses; a retargeted, removed, wrong-bank, shape-less or one-sided call refuses; the call checks are shape and operand checks, not fingerprints, so a changed call that keeps its shape is not noticed. Vanilla and its Lunar Magic resave give the same areas and cameras as before; the 4 hacks still refuse at the camera read.
