### Changed

- Maps view: the Podoboo ($33) is now drawn with its own art. Its graphics routine points the player tile upload at the player graphics buffer (slot 3 of both rows: chars 6, 7, $16, $17 from buffer +$6600 and +$6800, SMWDisX bank_01.asm:13180-13197, bank_00.asm:4583-4605), so the chars it uses are not the level's SP1 sheet, which the map view drew before. HackBench builds that buffer from the ROM (GFX33 expanded 3bpp to 4bpp, then GFX32, bank_00.asm:6250-6290) and reads the pointers from the routine's own bytes; a ROM whose routine or boot code differs marks the Podoboo with the reason instead of drawing it. Evidence, vanilla ROM, one machine: the decoded buffer equals the chars the hardware holds at Mario's own pose pointers in 3732 of 3864 capture samples (the rest are samples between a pose change and its upload), the Podoboo's four chars equal the hardware VRAM in 240 of 240 samples that show them (8 maps; no sample has the Podoboo's pointers in its WRAM, so the chars are the witness), and 263 distinct expanded GFX33 tiles occur verbatim in 6829 VRAM samples. Over all 3387 sprites of the vanilla maps only the 72 Podoboos changed, in pixels; no box or status changed.
