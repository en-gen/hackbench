### Changed

- Maps view: the Podoboo ($33) is now drawn with its own art. Its graphics routine points the player tile upload at the player graphics buffer (slot 3 of both rows: chars 6, 7, $16, $17 from buffer +$6600 and +$6800, SMWDisX bank_01.asm:13180-13197, bank_00.asm:4583-4605), so the chars it uses are not the level's SP1 sheet, which the map view drew before. HackBench builds that buffer from the ROM (GFX33 expanded 3bpp to 4bpp, then GFX32, bank_00.asm:6250-6290) and takes the slots from the pointers the Podoboo's own run left in WRAM, not from the routine's bytes. A ROM whose boot routine is not the stock one (141 of the 146 hack-corpus ROMs) keeps the map's SP1 art for the Podoboo and says why in a note. Evidence, vanilla ROM, one machine: the decoded buffer equals the chars the hardware holds at Mario's own pose pointers in 3732 of 3864 capture samples (the rest are samples between a pose change and its upload), the Podoboo's four chars equal the hardware VRAM in 240 of 240 samples that show them (8 maps), all 72 Podoboos of the 12 vanilla maps that have one equal the hardware chars laid out by their part offsets, pixel for pixel and color for color, and 263 distinct expanded GFX33 tiles occur verbatim in 6829 VRAM samples. Over all 3387 sprites of the vanilla maps only the 72 Podoboos changed, in pixels; no box or status changed.
- Sprite report grader: the level's cluster sprites (the castle flames) no longer draw into the tested sprite's OAM, as the map view already ensured (#809). Over the 1,957 captured records (one machine, one run, commit 17dbd4d0) exact went 998 to 1018, wrong 125 to 97, empty 13 to 14; 28 records changed grade, 27 of them for the better and one (map 101 `$54`, whose only recorded tile is a castle-flame piece) from wrong to empty.
