### Changed

- Play and simulate: the line-guided sprites ($62, $64, $65, $66, $67, $68) now take the pick the grinder has (#126): the run is placed where the line walk has put the sprite on its third frame, not at its spawn spot or the probe-corner spot between (#792). Why: every one of these ids draws before its line step in the shared line-guide dispatch (SMWDisX bank_01.asm:12182-12204, platform arm 12323-12358), and the step then snaps the sprite onto its line (bank_01.asm:12054-12061). Effect on the vanilla ROM, over all its placements (7 maps, one machine, 2026-10-20): 4 of 6 $62, 24 of 32 $65, the one $66, 17 of 20 $67 and 28 of 37 $68 move off the generic rule's spot, by up to 16 px (a $62 by 5 px, a $68 by 4 or 11); each one that moved sits on a line tile (Map16 $76-$99), and on a straight line tile ($90-$93) at its edge. The 12 $64 ropes (D85 call 1) stay where they were, on the top edge of a $92 line tile. $63 is unchanged. A sprite whose third pass draws nothing keeps its first drawn pass (only the grinder stops at the second).
- Maps view: the picture of those sprites at rest is measured from the position where the draw ran, not from where the pass ended (draw, then move). On the vanilla ROM this moves 6 of 6 $62 by +4,+4 px, 31 of 32 $65 and 36 of 37 $68 by +4 or -4 across and +4 down, the one $66 by +4,+4 and all 20 $67 likewise; the 12 ropes do not move (same machine and date).
- Maps view: a chainsaw, grinder or fuzz ball ($65-$68) is drawn where its own start-up code leaves it (320 px left of an even column, 15 px right of an odd one), worked out by running the ROM's code, not from a constant (#792, R16). The tile the map stores it on is outlined while it is selected, and the Properties panel says how far it moved. A sprite whose start-up code does not move it stays on its tile.
- Properties preview: a chainsaw no longer drifts along its track; every frame keeps one anchor (#1128).
- Line-guided call targets are named by a fingerprint of the routine read from the ROM, not by vanilla addresses (#1121), with the FastROM bit of long operands folded; the interact slot takes any callee that is not a draw or the line step. An edited stock routine or start-up routine is flagged unverified.
