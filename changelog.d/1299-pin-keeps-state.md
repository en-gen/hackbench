### Changed

- Double-clicking the map a preview tab is showing pins it with that preview's palace and switch presses and its layer, sprite, grid and collision toggles. Double-clicking another map's row carries the toggles but starts the presses unpressed (#1274). Pressing Enter on a filtered row for another map pins it with everything at its defaults. A map that already has a pinned tab keeps its own. Zoom and scroll are not carried: the pinned tab opens fitted at the map's start (#1299).
