### Changed

- The sprite "Unverified" note now reads in plain words, for example "Unverified: 2 sprites may not be drawn exactly as in the game.", and says "1 sprite" for one. The technical cause moves to the tooltip, which lists every distinct cause instead of the first; the Sprites button's tooltip lists them all too. The Properties panel shows the same plain line for a selected sprite ("this sprite may not be drawn exactly as in the game"), with the cause as a tooltip and, by keyboard or touch, in a section that opens under the line (#1166).
