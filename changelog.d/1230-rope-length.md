### Changed

- Map view (#1230, D152): the rope ($64) draws at the length the game gives it. The map view runs the ROM's own rope draw in the slot the level loader would pick (read from the loader's tables, bank_02.asm:5376-5405) instead of slot 0, so on sprite memory setting 1 a rope in an odd column (or odd row on a vertical map) draws 9 tiles and one in an even column 5, and setting 0 always 5. A loader that is not the original keeps the 5-tile rope and the Properties panel says why in a plain-words line. Settings 2 to 10 and 12 to 16, where the rope is long only if a high sprite slot is free at spawn, draw the short rope for now; the extra length comes later. Vanilla ROM, map $00F, one machine: columns 205 and 241 draw 9 tiles and 164, 186, 210, 216 draw 5, matching the Mesen capture (4 of 4 runs).
