### Changed

- Map view (#1231, #1176, D154): every sprite is now drawn where its own setup code (INIT) leaves it, not re-based onto the tile the level stores it on, so a Piranha Plant is centered on its pipe (+8, -1) and the brown chained platform ($5F) hangs from its block (+120, +104). The stored tile is a dashed square, shown only where the drawing does not cover it; clicking it selects its sprite, even under another sprite's drawing, and selecting a sprite still outlines the tile solid. A moved sprite the game never shows in play stays on its stored tile, on evidence from the spawn (an empty spawn run alone is none, so static sprites on vertical maps are drawn). Vanilla ROM, one machine: 410 of 3239 sprites on the 512 maps draw off their stored tile (371 with a dashed square); the stored position of every sprite equals its list entry (24 maps give no sprite reply). Replaces D134, D135, D136 and the at-rest part of D91 (docs/decisions/2026-11-14-sprites-draw-where-their-code-puts-them.md).
