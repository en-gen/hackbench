### Added

- Maps view (#984): clicking a sprite opens a read-only Properties panel on the right (View > Properties reopens it) with the sprite's id, name, position, each drawn part (tile, palette row, flips, offset), the palette rows used and a preview of the sprite. A sprite with two or more distinct poses (a Koopa, a Rex) loops them in the preview, about 7 times a second, from frames the app runs on demand; one with a single pose says "Not animated"; one the app cannot animate says "Animation not shown" and why. Empty ground or closing the map shows "No sprite selected". Evidence: unit tests on synthetic sprites; the Playwright spec for a vanilla map was written but not run by the author.
