### Changed

- A sprite that is drawn when the map opens but disappears when Mario or a switch changes is now tried against all six reveal conditions (Mario at, left and right of it, more frames, the P-switch, the ON/OFF switch), not only the three Mario positions. A condition that only draws the same picture as when the map opens is not counted as a reveal (#1365). On the vanilla ROM, the 29 moles on maps 10 and 106 now show in the screen door with "is not always there, and the app could not tell what hides it" instead of "appears when Mario gets close to this spot".
