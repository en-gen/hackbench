### Changed

- Sprite and collision previews: the level loader now recognises seven of the game's loader spans (each hiding at least 24 unknown bytes, widened over the whole routine rather than hashed short) and checks the jump into the pointer loader by its target by a SHA-256 fingerprint instead of a copy of their bytes, so the repository no longer holds ROM code slices there. Nothing changes on the vanilla ROM: the loader output for all 512 level numbers (steps and the full work RAM image) hashes identically before and after, one machine, 2026-10-17. A ROM whose routine differs is still refused with the same plain-words reason naming the routine; a test per routine changes one byte and expects that refusal.
