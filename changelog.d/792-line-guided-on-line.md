### Changed

- The map view draws line-guided sprites (the chainsaws, grinders, fuzzies, ropes and line platforms) where the game first has them on their line, instead of beside it (#792, D156). A saw or grinder the game never shows (it starts off the left of the screen as the map scrolls right) stays on its own tile and the Properties panel says it never appears in play. The shift note now says the sprite is drawn where it first sits on its line.
