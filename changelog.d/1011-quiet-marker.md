### Changed

- Quiet marker for placed items the game draws nothing for (#1011): shooters, generators, scroll commands and sprites whose run finishes empty show a thin ring with a center dot you can see the tile through, with a plain-words tooltip, instead of the framed id box. Refused or failed sprites keep the framed box. The class is read by running the game's own sprite-list routine from the ROM on the built-in 65816 core, cached per ROM version; checked on the vanilla and Magic ROMs and three hacks, and refused (framed box kept, marked unverified) where the routine cannot be followed or proves nothing; three hacks that change it read the same table. Where the loader cannot be read, every sprite with extra bit 2 or 3 is boxed with the reason "loader unreadable" instead of being drawn with vanilla art.
