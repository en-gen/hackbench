### Changed

- The map view no longer adds an empty row (or column) for a sprite whose art spills past the map's edge (#1325, owner ruling 2026-11-07). On vanilla (sprite sweep of 488 maps, one machine, develop dfb9d43a): $00E, $01B, $0F8 and $110 lose the 16 px row below the map; $0D7 loses 8 px above; nine maps lose the 8 px column on the left; and $0F8 also loses its 32 px right-side band (ring $E2 art), so its far end is the gutter alone. The art is cut at the map's edge and the sprite still hovers and selects from the part on the map. A sprite or refused-object mark placed off the map still grows the canvas and shows there, a sprite whose art touches neither the map nor its own placement cell stays drawn and selectable, and the far-end gutter is unchanged.
