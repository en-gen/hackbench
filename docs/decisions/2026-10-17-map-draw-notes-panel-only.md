# Draw notes appear only in the Properties panel

## Question

Notes about how the map is drawn (layer, sprite, block and general notes) are lines above the map today. Do they move into the Properties panel, or stay?

## Options considered

1. Move them into the panel only.
2. Keep the lines above the map and also list them in Notes and Problems. The mockup proposed this.

## Ruling

Option 1. "D95B" (Brian, 2026-10-19): drawing notes appear only in the map Properties panel, with no lines over the map.

## Why

The owner chose the clean map: nothing is drawn over it for these notes, and the panel lists them. The mockup had proposed option 2 on the grounds that they qualify the picture; that was not adopted.
