# Draw notes stay above the map and are also listed

## Question

Notes about how the map is drawn (layer, sprite, block and general notes) are lines above the map today. Do they move into the Properties panel, or stay?

## Options considered

1. Move them into the panel only.
2. Keep the lines above the map and also list them in Notes and Problems.

## Ruling

wording not recorded. Option 2 is what the mockup shows and the owner picked (D111, 2026-10-17). `[INF]` Not ruled as a call; owner review pending.

## Why

They qualify the picture, so they belong next to it. Listing them as well keeps the count and Problems complete and leaves nothing only in a tooltip.
